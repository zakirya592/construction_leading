Questions

Before you break ground.

Straight answers on timing, cost and what happens after the keys are handed over.

How long does a typical project take?

A custom home is often 10–16 months. A commercial building runs 14–30 months depending on height, permits and fit-out. We give you a fixed schedule at the end of planning, then report against it every week.

How do you price the work?

Open-book, fixed price once the drawings are set. You see labor, materials and our fee as separate lines. Changes are written up before anyone starts, so the number you approved is the number you pay.

Where do you build?

Portland and the surrounding region for ground-up work, and selective projects across the Northwest when the scope fits our crews. Infrastructure work follows the agencies we already partner with.

What happens after handover?

You receive drawings, warranties and a walkthrough with the people who built it. Structural work carries a 10-year warranty, and the same project director stays on call for the first year.
